# WCPG 2026 poster resources

## Psychiatric-cognitive overlap is locus-specific and disorder-dependent

This page accompanies the Project 2 poster presented at WCPG 2026. The primary
analysis uses European-ancestry data; the East Asian analysis is exploratory.

Abbreviations: MDD, major depressive disorder; SCZ, schizophrenia; EA,
educational attainment; CF, cognitive function.

## Poster results

Genome-wide LDSC estimates showed negative genetic correlations for MDD with
EA and CF. The SCZ-EA estimate was close to zero, although the regional results
contained effects in both directions.

| Trait pair | Genome-wide genetic correlation | Negative local correlations | Positive local correlations |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MDD-EA | -0.279 | 179 | 18 |
| MDD-CF | -0.211 | 74 | 7 |
| SCZ-EA | 0.024 | 28 | 58 |
| SCZ-CF | -0.243 | 64 | 7 |

The local counts are FDR-significant LAVA results. Their totals match the
public [LAVA test-count record](provenance/lava_pair_test_counts.tsv).

PLEIO identified 998 four-trait loci, including 244 loci detected only in the
joint analysis. The poster summarises their directional composition as:

| Model | Concordant | Discordant | Other or complex |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MDD | 50.3% | 13.6% | 36.1% |
| SCZ | 31.0% | 24.9% | 44.1% |

Here, concordant loci have psychiatric-risk effects aligned with lower EA or
CF. Discordant loci have psychiatric-risk effects aligned with higher EA or
CF. The published classification code also retains Dual, Mixed and Unassigned
labels before the poster categories are combined.

Functional follow-up found cell-adhesion and cadherin-related enrichment in
selected discordant and SCZ locus sets. The underlying terms are available in
the [processed g:Profiler results](results/gprofiler/processed/significant_terms.tsv).
