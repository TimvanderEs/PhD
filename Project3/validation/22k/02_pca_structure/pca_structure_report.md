# Combined 22k PCA structure

The three latency indicators retained the largest loadings. Their 95% bootstrap intervals were Reaction Time **[0.428, 0.450]**, Stroop Box **[0.545, 0.557]**, and Stroop Ink **[0.479, 0.494]**.

Leave-one-task-out scores correlated **r = 0.947–0.986** with the full PC1. Loading congruence was **0.999–1.000** across recorded ancestry groups and **0.998–1.000** across HELIOS10K and HELIOS20K.

After replacing Reaction Time, Stroop Box, and Stroop Ink with one speed-domain indicator, the four-indicator PC1 explained **39.39%** of variance and correlated **r = 0.931** with the six-task PC1.
